Escalation Workflow

From signal ingestion to resolution: how the engine routes critical risk items to the right team, tracks acknowledgement, and alerts on SLA breaches.

1. Signal Ingested

New article or event enters the pipeline after RSS fetch, parsing, and geo-resolution.

2. Rule Match

Escalation rules evaluate category, country, severity, and source against configured triggers.

3. Escalation Created

A case is opened with owner, SPOC, priority, and SLA deadline automatically assigned.

4. Alert & Acknowledge

Notifications route to the right team. Acknowledgement stops the SLA clock.

5. SLA Breach Guard

Missed deadlines surface in red and can cascade to secondary channels or leadership.

6. Resolve

Analysts add notes, change status, and close the case with a full audit trail.
